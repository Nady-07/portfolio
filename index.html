About Me

Manufacturing Analytics & Commercial Sales Experience

I am a Junior Data Analyst with eight months of hands-on manufacturing analytics experience and a strong background in customer-facing sales. As the sole Data Analyst at Arab Company for Calcium Carbonate, I handled end-to-end data preparation, cleaned and validated Excel source data, built Power BI reports with both desktop and mobile layouts, and communicated directly with the CEO and factory owner.

Prior to transitioning into dedicated analytics, I worked in Cairo's real estate sector at Smart Step and MLS, maintaining property listings, customer follow-up trackers, and Excel sales performance summaries.

My portfolio work covers SQL Server analysis, customer segmentation, multi-year sales trends, and customer churn. I am focused on helping a Growth or BI team turn customer and business data into clear, measurable actions.
